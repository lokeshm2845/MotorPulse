How it works

An AI stethoscope for machines, built for farms with weak networks and budget phones.

1

Enrol

Record the pump 3 times while it is healthy. That becomes its own sound fingerprint.

2

Check

Hold the phone at the same spot for 10 seconds. Bad recordings are rejected with guidance.

3

Understand

Health 0–100, likely cause, what to do and a typical repair cost — spoken aloud.

4

Act early

Trends catch slow decline; one tap shares the report with a mechanic.

Clear verdicts, not charts

Every check ends in one of three answers a farmer can act on.

HealthyThe pump sounds like its normal self. Check again in a week.
WatchSomething changed. Simple do-now advice and a re-check in 2–3 days.
Act nowLikely cause such as bearing wear or dry running, with steps to prevent bigger damage.
